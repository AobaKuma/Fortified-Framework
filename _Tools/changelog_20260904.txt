[h1]Update 2026-09-04[/h1]
[i]Changes since the 2026-08-20 release (15 commits).[/i]

[h2]New features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Mech modification window[/b] — every player mech now gets a [b]Mech modification[/b] gizmo (once microelectronics is researched) that opens a dedicated screen: body slots on the left, compatible modifications available on the map in the middle, and a queue of pending install / remove operations on the right. Click an available modification to queue an install, the [b]X[/b] on an installed one to queue its removal, and double-click an installed one to open its info card. [b]Apply[/b] hands the queue to the mech itself, which walks over, picks the parts up and installs them as ordinary queued jobs; [b]Reset[/b] clears the queue. Anything that cannot be queued says why (not enough reachable items, slot already occupied, unique modification, stack limit reached).
[*][b]Modification presets[/b] — the planned queue can be saved to disk and replayed on another mech, so a squad can be outfitted the same way without rebuilding the list every time. Entries whose body part or item does not exist on the new mech are skipped instead of failing the whole preset. Presets live in [code]FFF_MechModificationPresets[/code] under the save-data folder, and presets written by the old DMS mech customization window ([code].dmsmc[/code]) are still readable.
[*][b]Stackable modifications[/b] — a modification whose hediff supports merging can now be installed more than once on the same slot, up to a limit the modification itself declares. Protective shields use this: the cap scales with the mech's body size, and each extra unit adds to the shield pool. Modifications that do not opt in are still strictly one per slot.
[*][b]Research discovery (opt-in)[/b] — the new [b]ModExtension_ResearchDiscovery[/b] can be attached to a ResearchTabDef (whole tab) or a single ResearchProjectDef (override). Projects whose prerequisites are not yet complete are treated as [i]undiscovered[/i]: they show as unknown research, cannot be selected or started, are excluded from quick search, and books cannot pour progress into them. A letter announces the projects that become available. Tabs and projects without the extension are completely unaffected.
[*][b]Archived technology unsealing items[/b] — new [b]CompProperties_UseEffect_DiscoverResearch[/b] makes a usable item permanently reveal a random sealed project from its list and inject research progress into it. Works for both normal and knowledge-cost projects. If everything on the list is already finished (or the item is configured to gamble), it falls back to granting intellectual experience instead of doing nothing.
[*][b]Drone platforms can deploy several unit types[/b] — [b]spawnPawnKinds[/b] lets a platform list multiple deployable models, each with its own material cost, units per deployment, cooldown, gizmo icon, label and required research. A [b]Deploy type[/b] gizmo switches between them, locked entries show what research they need, and NPC platforms pick a model by weight. The old single [b]spawnPawnKind[/b] setting still works unchanged.
[*][b]Autonomous worktables can feed themselves[/b] — with [code]pullFromLinkedStorage[/code] enabled, a machine pulls recipe ingredients straight out of linked storage buildings and starts the bill on its own, no hauler required. Source buildings opt in explicitly via the new [b]ModExtension_MaterialSource[/b] (with options for working while unpowered and for respecting colonist reservations), so shelves and cabinets are never raided by surprise. Bills with a pawn / slave / mech restriction are deliberately left alone, and the machine must meet the recipe's skill requirements through its own rated skills.
[*][b]Artificial military organisms (AMO)[/b] — new [b]ArtificialOrganism[/b] and [b]ArtificialOrganismHumanlike[/b] pawn classes with [b]CompArtificialOrganism[/b]: they slowly regenerate their own injuries and convert stored rations into energy through the new [b]IRationSource[/b] interface, and are directly controllable without a mechanitor. They cannot be repaired by mechanitors or overseers — the repair job, the overseer float menu option and the self-repair think node all refuse them.
[*][b]Air support can be remembered[/b] — [b]AirSupportDef[/b] gained [code]taleOnTriggered[/code] and [code]historyEventOnTriggered[/code], recorded at the single point every call path funnels through. Only calls made by player-faction pawns are recorded, so enemy or building-triggered strikes do not pollute the art and ideoligion systems.
[/list]

[h2]Overseers — reworked[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Bandwidth, control groups and command range are now stats[/b] instead of comp settings, so they can be modified by the usual stat machinery. Command range uses the new [b]FFF_MechCommandRange[/b] stat, and bandwidth and control groups are listed on the building's stat panel.
[*][b]Overseer facilities[/b] — the new [b]CompOverseerFacility[/b] lets support buildings raise an overseer's stats through [code]statOffsets[/code]. Bandwidth is recalculated when a facility is linked, unlinked or loses power, so the mech count updates immediately instead of after a reload.
[*][b]Power now gates the overseer[/b] — an unpowered overseer building stops commanding, and control returns the moment power comes back, both on power signals and on load.
[*][b]Bandwidth recache on equipment change[/b] — an overseer mech that gains or loses apparel refreshes its bandwidth immediately.
[*]The command-range ring is only drawn when the range is actually finite, and the "select mech to control" targeting command has its own icon.
[/list]

[h2]Fixes[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Modification installs could consume the wrong item — or nothing at all[/b]. Install and remove jobs now carry the exact target body part, hediff and item def instead of relying on a flag on the hediff. The job re-resolves and re-reserves its material when it starts (falling back to any other reachable stack of the same item), re-validates the slot right before applying, and returns the carried item instead of destroying it when the operation is no longer valid.
[*][b]Removing a modification could remove the wrong one[/b] — with several modifications installed, the removal job targeted whichever one had the apply flag set. It now resolves the specific hediff and body part it was ordered against.
[*][b]"Not enough items" when the item was in hand[/b] — using a modification item directly on a mech no longer runs a map-wide reservation check that hid the very stack being used.
[*][b]Modification slot checks[/b] — occupancy is judged by actual modification hediffs rather than any hediff on the part, missing body parts are excluded, and the whole utility path is null-safe instead of logging errors on a null pawn or comp.
[*][b]Protective shields[/b] — hit points are clamped into range on every write (previously an over-cap value was written straight through), the maximum is rounded and floored at 1 so a tiny body size can no longer produce a divide-by-zero, and merging a shield from another source no longer throws when the incoming hediff has no shield comp.
[*][b]Autonomous worktable ate its ingredients into limbo[/b] — finished bills cleared the container instead of destroying its contents, leaving ownerless things behind and skipping comp cleanup. Ingredients are now destroyed the way vanilla does it.
[*][b]Autonomous worktable crashes[/b] — a table without a power comp no longer throws every 250 ticks, cancelling a bill on an unspawned (minified or under-construction) table no longer throws, and a [code]workAmountPerStage[/code] of 0 no longer turns the remaining-stage count into garbage.
[*][b]Colonist and machine fighting over the same bill[/b] — a colonist arriving with ingredients can no longer overwrite a bill the machine already started (which reset all accumulated work), and a delivery job aborts cleanly when the machine started on its own instead of tipping a second set of ingredients into the container to be eaten.
[*][b]Drone refund exploit[/b] — retracting a drone refunded the cost of the model currently selected on the platform, so deploying a cheap model, switching the selector and retracting minted free materials. The refund is now always calculated from the retracted drone's own model.
[*][b]Drone refunds fell on the floor[/b] — a refund larger than the stack limit under-paid, and a nearly full container dumped the entire refund on the ground. The container now takes whatever fits and only the true overflow is dropped.
[*][b]Over-filled drone platforms[/b] — the "release excess materials" path compared stack count instead of total materials, so it never ran; when forced to run it dropped things that were not in the container and could loop forever. It is rewritten to work on the real material total.
[*][b]Mech command range[/b] — the range check now reads the overseer's command-range stat, and every state-controllable mech (overseers, AMOs) is recognised through one interface instead of scattered type checks.
[*][b]Drones could not be ordered to fire[/b] — vanilla disables the ranged attack gizmo for any mech without an overseer subject comp, which drones do not have. A narrowly scoped transpiler re-enables it at that one call site for player-owned mechs only; enemy, wild and enslaved drones stay blocked, and the global controllability rule is untouched.
[*][b]Mech-only colonies could not reform a caravan[/b] — the reform command is now offered when the map has no free colonists but does have player-faction pawns capable of caravanning, with the usual hostile-threat, gravship and shuttle confirmations.
[*][b]Overseer dummy pawn[/b] — a failed dummy generation or a dummy hediff replaced by another mod now logs once and disables that overseer instead of throwing on a vanilla notification path.
[*][b]Traditional Chinese substation text[/b] — the status line no longer claims a hard-coded 3000 W output, and the EMP line now reads as protection rather than "disabled".
[/list]

[h2]Under the hood[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Cached mech comps[/b] — [b]ICachedMechComps[/b] gives WeaponUsableMech, HumanlikeMech and ArtificialOrganism cached access to their overseer-subject, dead-man-switch, command-relay, drone and repairable comps. Harmony patches on hot paths (command range, drafting, weapon dropping, player control) now read the cache instead of running a fresh comp lookup every call.
[*][b]Two new interfaces replace hard-coded type checks[/b] — [b]IStateControllableMech[/b] is the single answer to "can this thing be controlled directly right now", and [b]IHumanlikeMech[/b] marks anything with humanlike initialisation, rendering and apparel support. Rendering, apparel, gear-tab and generation patches now test the interface, so other mods can supply their own classes without inheriting ours.
[*][b]HumanlikeMechUtility[/b] — the tracker setup, head graphic and work-type restriction logic moved out of HumanlikeMech into a shared utility that any IHumanlikeMech implementation can call.
[*][b]Modification item index[/b] — a new map component tracks item stacks so the modification window and its jobs can count and locate materials without scanning the map each frame.
[*][b]Local build tooling[/b] — [code]_Tools/BuildDLL[/code] builds every project of the mod (and its siblings) in dependency order, refuses to overwrite a DLL while RimWorld has it loaded, and condenses the compiler output into a grouped BuildReport.
[*]New defs: [b]FFF_ModificationRemove[/b] JobDef and the [b]FFF_MechCommandRange[/b] StatDef; new overseer targeting icon; English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese text for the modification window, drone deployment types, overseer control and research discovery.
[/list]

[h2]For mod authors[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]CompProperties_Overseer[/b] no longer reads [code]commandRange[/code], [code]controlGroups[/code] or [code]bandwidth[/code]. Move those values to the building's [code]statBases[/code] as [b]FFF_MechCommandRange[/b], [b]MechControlGroups[/b] and [b]MechBandwidth[/b].
[*][b]Storage buildings[/b] must declare [b]ModExtension_MaterialSource[/b] before an autonomous worktable will pull from them, and the facility link itself still has to be set up on both sides in the usual way.
[/list]
